## Latency scenarios

The bridge configured round-trip delays of 0, 80, 150, 250 and 400 ms. Nonzero delay scenarios included 20 ms jitter and 1% loss on replaceable input/world packets. Critical action messages remained ordered/reliable. For each delay, the tests checked immediate local key/input registration, bounded vehicle state, host/guest convergence after release, stale sequence rejection, and finite state through sustained driving. At 400 ms, a large authority relocation plus damage exercised hard correction, and a pickup action was verified not to execute twice.

The 400 ms hard-correction sample ended with a 1.224 m guest/host gap immediately after correction under continued input; convergence after release passed the scenario bound. This is simulated application behavior, not measured field RTT or proof of lower network RTT. No claim is made that real RTT decreased.

The key-response assertion checks registration and prediction arming in the same browser event turn; it does not measure display scanout or human-perceived input-to-photon time. The test bridge is not WebRTC, ICE or a Cloudflare relay test.

## TURN diagnostic coverage

Synthetic relay candidates exercise six separate relay-only PeerConnections. Only `typ relay` before the 7 second deadline is accepted. A near-deadline/late candidate does not win recommendation, and the highlighted row and saved/displayed recommendation use the same route. Temporary mock credentials were absent from UI and local storage. No real Cloudflare candidate or live device pair was exercised in this run.
