# V0.8.0 2v2 PK — implementation notes

## Match rules

- Four players are split by room slot: players 1–2 are the amber truck; players 3–4 are the teal truck. The first player on each team drives; the second handles the cargo bay.
- Both teams compete to deliver more parcels in five minutes. The host owns scoring, projectile hits, damage, repair progress, and the match clock. At the limit, the higher delivery count wins; a tie starts up to 60 seconds of sudden-death overtime, where the next delivery wins.
- The cargo-bay lever fires one ordinary parcel. Special/order-bound parcels are not cannon ammunition. Each shot consumes that parcel and the cannon has a five second cooldown. A hit slows the target for three seconds. The third hit disables and ignites the truck.
- Fire removes 1.2% vehicle health per second and accumulates across extinguishing. At 30 cumulative burning seconds the truck explodes and its team forfeits. The fire extinguisher must complete a 2.5 second action before the wrench can start its four second repair. Repair does not erase accumulated fire time, so a later fire resumes the same 30 second fuse.
- Match actions carry unique event IDs and are accepted once by the host. Clients only render the host-confirmed match state.

## Current implementation boundary

`src/duel2v2-rules.js` owns two vehicles, team cargo counts, deliveries, shots, hit state, repairs, and match end. `src/duel2v2-runtime.js` adds the four-player room choice, isolated room IDs, public room mode selection, invitation mode parameters, host input/action routing, 2D HUD, visible second van and cargo, and city-target delivery loop. `build.py` embeds both modules into the single-file candidate. The city movement uses a soft road boundary; this pass does not add physical city/traffic collision response to the second van. The candidate has not had four-client Chromium or real-device acceptance yet.
